## Platform

web

## Users

Macojaune's visitors discover his photography, follow his projects, buy prints, and take part in participatory projects. Cho Kaché is for people in Guadeloupe who encounter a hidden print or follow clues online to look for one.

## Product Purpose

Macojaune.com publishes Marvin's photographic work and supports direct relationships around that work. Cho Kaché turns selected physical prints into free objects hidden in Guadeloupe. Success means that a finder understands the rules, documents the discovery if they want to, takes the print, and lets the site record that it has been found.

Maco'misyon gathers selected projects into a navigable world where their real progress can become visible. A visitor can understand a project, explore its place in the world and later see what collective actions changed.

## Positioning

Cho Kaché links a numbered physical photograph, a real hiding place, public clues, and a unique scan page. The print itself is the object won by the finder.

Maco'misyon links the projects Marvin chooses to share, their concrete stages and their visible consequences.

## Operating Context

Macojaune hides prints during outings, events, and trips. People learn about them through his social posts or encounter them by chance. Each print is accompanied by a monochrome thermal-printer ticket carrying the Cho Kaché name, a QR code, and the Macojaune handle.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is a Nuxt application backed by markdown content, TinaCMS, Turso, Cloudflare R2, Stripe, and Umami.
- Three Cho Kaché prints are already hidden.
- An unfound print can expose a public location or keep its location hidden.
- The exact locations and visibility choices for the current three prints have not yet been supplied in the repository.
- The existing article at `/blog/cho-kache` explains the origin and general rules of the project.
- Finding a print must remain possible without creating an account.
- `/macomisyon` begins with a simplified isometric map of Jarry around La Jaille, then opens the QuiLivreOù warehouse called Dépôt Q.
- The first map integration uses demonstrative data. Real counts, Zikak, notifications and the private control panel come later.
- The map must remain usable on a phone, explain projects without relying on WebGL and respect reduced-motion settings.

## Brand Commitments

- The project name is `Cho Kaché`, the Creole name for hide-and-seek. URLs remain unaccented as `cho-kache`.
- The existing ticket is monochrome, direct, typographic, and headed by `An nou joue CHO KACHE`.
- The voice uses direct second-person French and can include Guadeloupean Creole.
- The Macojaune site already uses black, amber, white, Tanker for display type, and Space Grotesk for body text.

## Product Principles

- Explain permission plainly: the finder may take the print.
- Keep hidden locations genuinely hidden in every public response and page source.
- Treat sharing a photo or video as an invitation, not a condition for receiving the print.
- Show the live state of each print without spoiling active secret caches.
- Make the scan useful in a few seconds on a phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The page must remain understandable without relying on map position, color, animation, or social media participation alone.
