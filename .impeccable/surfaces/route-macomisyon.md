# Maco'misyon surface brief

## Scope and mode

This brief governs `/macomisyon`. Its mode is Experience. In a few seconds, a visitor should recognise a small fictional Jarry, notice that projects live there and understand that QuiLivreOù can be entered.

## Audience, job and first delivery

The route is for people who follow Marvin's projects and for people who arrive through one of those projects. It must work as an introduction without requiring knowledge of the word Komisyon. This first delivery contains the Jarry map and a static but living version of Dépôt Q. It does not claim to show live counters.

## Direction

Keep the Macojaune black, amber, Tanker and Space Grotesk system. The 3D map is a stylised isometric miniature, never a GIS recreation or a dashboard. La Jaille, road loops, the Voie Verte, industrial volumes and the sea make Jarry recognisable. The QuiLivreOù hangar is the first warm, active point. Future projects remain quiet markers in the distance.

The page frames the world as a game console. Labels are short, square and physical. A selected place opens a compact dossier that can be read on a phone. The canvas is never the only route to the content. The inherited Depot Q palette extends the portfolio palette locally: dark green controls, mint completed states, warm paper, amber actions and turquoise water. These scene colors do not replace the global portfolio design.

## Motion and accessibility

The island has restrained life: vehicles move slowly, water shifts and a selected hangar emits a readable signal. Reduced motion stops travel and uses an immediate camera change. Keyboard users can select QuiLivreOù through an ordinary button. If WebGL fails, a textual project index remains available.

## States and unresolved decisions

QuiLivreOù is the only enterable project in this release. Shootareas and Zikak can be selected to read their clues, but their interiors stay closed. The true global mission data, project positions and thresholds remain open decisions. The initial camera shows the whole territory on desktop and a closer explorable view on mobile. Returning from the depot restores the camera framing saved before entry.
