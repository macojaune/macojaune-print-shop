# Club Komisyon — prompt de génération

Outil : image_gen intégré. Références Macojaune et QuiLivreOù inspectées ; planche nouvelle composée à partir du prompt.

Sortie native : 1536 × 1024 pixels, copiée sans modification des pixels vers `02-club-komisyon.png`.

Vérification visuelle : écran mobile dominant, deux projets accessibles, compteur collectif 7/10, action Participer, widget séparé, popup vidéo, tampon en trois temps, objet de bureau et palette présents. La typographie et les pictogrammes de projets sont des propositions graphiques illustratives ; ce raster ne constitue pas un jeu de polices, de logos ou de composants livrés pour production.

Use case: logo-brand / ui-mockup.
Create ONE polished art direction presentation board, landscape 3:2, very high fidelity with crisp typography, like a top independent branding studio presenting a complete visual system. Direction title: “CLUB KOMISYON”. It is an exploration board for Maco’misyon, a playful participatory creative universe from Macojaune. Everything shown is illustrative sample data.

BRAND: cream paper background, large purposeful flat areas of golden yellow #FBBF24 and ink black #111111. Small accents lagoon #2BB3A3 and orange-red. Street editorial print language, cut paper, die-cut stickers, offset black shadows, clean graphic collage, deliberate whitespace. Bold condensed type with the personality of Tanker, slightly slanted custom letterforms. The recurring recognizable wordmark must read exactly “MACO’MISYON”. Supporting UI text is plain geometric sans similar to Space Grotesk. Use recurring × and ○ marks from Macojaune, framing corners and abstract photographic contact-sheet marks. Keep it fun, confident, physical, bright and editorial, never corporate SaaS.

COMPOSITION:
Top left: very large clean title “CLUB KOMISYON” and a distinctive MACO’MISYON wordmark treatment with generous margins.
Left column: a vertical social story/post mockup, a strong typographic poster saying “KOMISYON ACTIVÉE”, using yellow/black cut-paper collage, huge cropped geometric forms, abstract photographic frame marks, no people and no fake photograph of Marvin. A recurring small stamped ticket “MISSION REMPLIE” appears as a brand signature sample, visibly a reusable design component.
Dominant center: one large and truly readable portrait mobile interface shown straight-on. Black perimeter but no Apple branding, no notch dependence. This is the main object of the board, bigger than the secondary modules. Cream/yellow interface headed “MACO’MISYON”. Below “À VOUS DE JOUER”. First project card “QuiLivreOù”, “Objectif : 10 inscriptions”, very large counter “7/10”, a visibly seventy-percent-filled progress bar, and a prominent button “Participer”. Second project card “Shootareas”, status “Disponible”, button “Découvrir”. These are two projects simultaneously accessible. No adventure stages and no personal XP, ranking or level. Avoid excessive small text. The UI must feel like a lively participation board, not a finance dashboard.
Right column: a detached torn-paper widget/ticket reading “QuiLivreOù” and “7/10 inscrits” with one clear progress bar. Beneath it, a separate reusable popup design in a contrasting color block with exact headline “VOUS AVEZ DÉBLOQUÉ LA VIDÉO” and button “Voir la vidéo”. It is a separate design specimen, not a claim that the 7/10 threshold has already triggered it.
Bottom right corner: a small three-quarter-view 3D desktop object, a playful sturdy cartridge-shaped tabletop screen in warm yellow plastic and black, showing the MACO’MISYON wordmark and the simple × ○ motif. It must remain subordinate to the large mobile screen, no game controller and no Apple device.
Footer: three small storyboard frames showing one stamp moving downward, contacting a card, and leaving the recurring “MISSION REMPLIE” impression; make this legible as a motion sequence. Four clean color swatches. Footer text exactly “EXPLORATION GRAPHIQUE · DONNÉES D’EXEMPLE”.

QUALITY AND CONSTRAINTS: coherent professional editorial grid, strong hierarchy, use the whole page without clutter. Cream substrate should be nearly clean, only subtle tactile paper texture. Authentic huge condensed headline lettering, punchy flat yellow, crisp dark outlines. UI labels are large and readable, not microtext. Respect all French spellings. No pixel art, no controller cliché, no fantasy RPG, no generic neon cyberpunk, no glassmorphism, no invented human identity, no fake real metrics, no QR code. This is a brand exploration raster board, not an implemented app.
